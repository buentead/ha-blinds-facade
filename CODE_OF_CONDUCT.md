# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes everyone who wants to use, discuss or improve it. We want
participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Home Assistant or KNX.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise ideas, not people.
- Stay on topic and assume good intent.
- Respect the time of maintainers and contributors: search existing issues first and
  include the information needed to reproduce a problem (blueprint version, Home
  Assistant version, logbook lines, trace).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, derogatory comments or personal attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or private
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces – issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews – and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer via the contact
options on the maintainer's profile of the hosting platform. All reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity, the
consequence ranges from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project.
